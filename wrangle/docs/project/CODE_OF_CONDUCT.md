# Code of conduct

Wrangle welcomes researchers and contributors of all backgrounds and levels of
technical experience. Participation includes issues, reviews, documentation and
other project spaces.

Treat people respectfully. Discuss the evidence and research need; explain
feedback in clear language. Make room for uncertainty and accessibility needs.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make personal attacks or disclose another
person's private information. Sexualized language and unwelcome sexual attention
are not acceptable. Criticism of code or a method must not become criticism of
a person's identity or competence.

Report a concern privately to mailme@mikkokotila.com. If the report concerns the
lead maintainer, contact another repository administrator through their published
contact route. Include only the information needed to investigate; do not publish
private reports or retaliate against a reporter.

Maintainers investigate fairly, limit disclosure to those needed to resolve the
matter, and explain actions to affected participants where possible. Responses
may include a requested correction, warning, temporary restriction or removal
from project spaces. Serious threats or repeated misconduct can lead directly
to removal. Decisions can be appealed to a different repository administrator.
